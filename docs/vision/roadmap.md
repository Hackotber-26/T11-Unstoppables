# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Simulation MVP

Build the initial demand, routing, and pooling model. Validate the product logic in a controlled simulation before live operations.

## Phase 2: Campus or controlled pilot

Run a small-scale implementation in a defined environment with limited operational complexity and direct supervision.

## Phase 3: Metro corridor pilot

Deploy at a transit corridor with meaningful rider flow, station clustering, and fleet support.

## Phase 4: Multi-station expansion

Add more stations, routes, and fleet partners while tuning pricing, matching, and dispatch policies.

## Phase 5: National or multi-city mobility platform

Expand from a station-centric design into a regional network of mobility coordination, enterprise partnerships, and urban analytics.

---

## Vertical themes across phases

- safety governance
- performance analytics
- rider trust and retention
- fleet utilization and driver satisfaction
- deployment maturity and observability
