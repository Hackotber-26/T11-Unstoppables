# Business Model

NimmaSaarthi is designed to produce economic value by improving ride utilization, reducing wasted vehicle movement, and aligning trip supply with predictable commuter demand.

---

## Revenue streams

### Platform commission
A percentage of pooled ride revenue generated through the platform.

### Subscription plans
Monthly commuter passes or bundled trip credits for recurring riders.

### Sponsored bays and pickup zones
Revenue from branded or premium pickup and waiting areas near transit nodes.

### Enterprise mobility partnership
Corporate or institutional mobility partnerships for commuter programs and employee transport benefits.

### Data and analytics services
Insights on commuter movement patterns, utilization, and corridor health can support planning and operations with appropriate governance.

---

## Value capture logic

The platform captures value by making each trip more efficient:

- fewer empty miles
- higher occupancy per vehicle
- smoother dispatch timing
- lower rider uncertainty
- more predictable driver earnings

This improves the economics of the system without forcing riders into a lower-quality experience.

---

## Cost structure

Major cost categories include:

- driver incentives and retention
- platform infrastructure and realtime operations
- safety operations and compliance
- customer support and dispute resolution
- analytics, simulation, and optimization tooling
- payment settlement and fraud monitoring

---

## Unit economics model

A healthy system should improve the following metrics:

- rider cost per trip
- average pickup wait time
- driver revenue per hour
- occupancy per trip
- average deadhead distance
- trip completion rate

If the platform can improve these metrics while preserving trust and safety, the model remains scalable and competitive.
