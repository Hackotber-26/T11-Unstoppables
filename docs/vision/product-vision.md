# Product Vision

## NimmaSaarthi

NimmaSaarthi is a metro-aware mobility orchestration platform that solves the connection problem between mass transit and the final destination.

---

## Guiding principle

Match journeys, not destinations.

---

## Core product goals

### Pool before arrival
Passengers should be grouped before they leave the station so the system can plan shared rides around corridor flows and timing.

### Coordinate vehicle supply
The platform should match available drivers and vehicles to station-side demand with awareness of trip timing, route shape, and expected drop sequences.

### Deliver transparent economics
Riders need pricing that reflects only their share of the trip, while drivers receive fair compensation for high-utilization dispatch.

### Create safe, reliable experiences
The experience should reduce uncertainty, support clear pickup logic, and surface safety policies transparently.

### Design for urban mobility networks
The architecture should work with transit nodes, fleets, and operational dashboards rather than treating mobility as a single isolated booking flow.

---

## Key features

### Pool Me
Request pooling before arrival and create a planned collective trip around common corridors.

### Journey matching
Use corridor-based and timing-aware matching instead of forcing identical end destinations.

### Vehicle assignment
Assign autos, cabs, shuttles, or other available vehicles according to route, capacity, and operational suitability.

### Fare engine
Use contribution-based or share-based pricing so riders pay for the trip they actually consume.

### SafHER
Integrate safety-first travel policies into the rider experience, dispatch operations, and trip review flow.

### WalkFlex
Create incentives for small walking segments to improve efficiency and reduce per-trip costs.

---

## Success measures

- higher pooling formation rate
- lower average rider cost per journey
- reduced wait time after station exit
- higher driver utilization and earnings
- fewer empty miles and lower congestion pressure
- improved rider retention and trust in mobility systems
