How Stops Are Ordered

Rather than treating each booking as an isolated point, the routing solver projects all passenger destinations onto forward geometric angle cones extending from the station exit.

Detour & Time Penalties

Every candidate stop must satisfy two strict mathematical inequalities:
\(\text{Detour}(Rider_i) \le 400\text{m}\) \(\Delta \text{Time}(Rider_i) \le 4.5\text{ mins}\) If adding a rider would cause excessive backtracking or delays for earlier passengers, the system rejects bundling them into this vehicle and queues them for another pod.
