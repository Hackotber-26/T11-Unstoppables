# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment for everyone who contributes to this project.

---

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive in discussions
- welcoming different perspectives and experiences
- offering and receiving feedback gracefully
- focusing on the health and safety of the platform and its users
- working toward better mobility outcomes for communities

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language
- personal attacks or intimidation
- disruptive, hostile, or retaliatory conduct
- attempts to exploit users, operators, or platform data

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct in a fair and consistent manner. Violations may result in warnings, removal from contribution channels, or other actions appropriate to the situation.

---

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that does not align with this code of conduct, report it privately to the maintainers through the project communication channels.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to interactions within the repository, issue tracker, discussions, and related project spaces.
