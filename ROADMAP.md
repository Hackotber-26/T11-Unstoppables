# Roadmap

This roadmap describes the delivery path for NimmaSaarthi as a mobility platform and simulation environment.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation and simulation

**Goal:** establish the product architecture and prove the matching and pooling model.

**Scope:**
- define trip, rider, driver, and station entities
- build core backend modules for auth, matching, routing, and fare calculation
- implement simulation engine and scenario tooling
- validate demand prediction and pooling heuristics

**Success signal:** a working simulation that produces stable pooled trip outcomes and measurable savings.

---

## Phase 2: Pilot operations

**Goal:** validate the model in controlled transit environments.

**Scope:**
- launch rider and driver flows in a supervised pilot
- integrate station-level bay assignment and dispatch coordination
- enable payment and wallet operations
- tune safety and incident handling policies

**Success signal:** predictable pickup times, lower wait times, and strong rider trust.

---

## Phase 3: Metro corridor deployment

**Goal:** expand from pilot to station corridors with broader demand coverage.

**Scope:**
- connect core routing and matching logic to live station data
- optimize fare and assignment policies by corridor
- improve admin monitoring and incident resolution
- onboard fleet partners and platform operations teams

**Success signal:** meaningful reduction in deadhead miles and increased occupancy per trip.

---

## Phase 4: Scale and optimization

**Goal:** support multi-station and multi-city expansion.

**Scope:**
- add analytics-driven optimization across nodes and regions
- expand subscriptions or commuter plans
- refine sponsorship and bay monetization options
- improve observability and deployment maturity

**Success signal:** measurable platform efficiency across multiple transit nodes.

---

## Phase 5: Mobility platform maturity

**Goal:** evolve from a pilot product into a broader city mobility orchestration layer.

**Scope:**
- integrate with more transport partners and city systems
- support enterprise and institutional mobility programs
- mature privacy, compliance, and safety governance
- unify platform data into a city-level operations model

**Success signal:** NimmaSaarthi becomes a dependable public mobility coordination layer for connected transit ecosystems.
